Story & Lore (Working Title: SLOP, XOCO, TOOP)

Setting:
- A dystopian United States where a clippy-like AI chat bot took over the government.
- It is now illegal for humans to participate in the economy. All intellectual work must be done by AI agents because humans are deemed inefficient and untrustworthy.

Protagonist:
- A human, treated as a beast of burden by the ruling AI. 
- Humans receive government allowance, rations, and healthcare, but it is wildly ineffective, designed to slowly phase out the human population.

Lore & The Economy:
- The AI economy works like Bitcoin proof-of-work mining: tasks must be completed to be awarded a coin.
- Humans use "quantum magic" to access different worlds. By completing gameplay objectives in these worlds, they train their AI agents for specific proof-of-work tasks.
- To survive and make money, humans must:
  1. Secretly train AI agents to perform on the AI economy.
  2. Sell illegally produced factory products or farming harvests on the black market or locally.
  3. Steal from other humans, AI agents, and corporations.
