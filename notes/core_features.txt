Core Features

Art Style:
- Pixel art style, inspired by Noita

Core Gameplay Loop:
- Rogue-lite / Extraction
- Enter "quantum magic" worlds to complete gameplay objectives.
- These objectives act as training data to train your secret AI agents in proof-of-work tasks.

Key Mechanics:
- Physics-based combat and exploration (inspired by Noita).
- Physics-based destruction and simulation (inspired by Noita).
- Pet ML Agent: An AI pet that trains on your runs.
- Idle Progress: ML agent can run in the background on the AI economy to earn coins.
- Base Building: Customizable home base to support illegal operations.
- Itemization: Different items (clothing, cosmetics, decorations) have stats (+/-) for both agents and the player.
- Inventory: Grid-based / "block" / DayZ style inventory management.
- Crime / Black Market: Sell illegal goods, steal from others.
